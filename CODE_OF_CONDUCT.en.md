# Code of conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

Contributors of all languages, abilities and experience levels are welcome. Discuss observable behavior in issues and reviews, respect disagreements and respond to specific feedback. Harassment, threats, personal attacks, doxxing and publication of someone else's private files or credentials are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or hide harmful content, close threads and restrict repeat offenders. Use GitHub's [Report abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) for confidential reports of harassment or privacy violations. Report software vulnerabilities through the [security channel](SECURITY.en.md).

# Tahm v0.9.0

[简体中文](v0.9.0.zh-CN.md) · [Download the portable Windows x64 build](https://github.com/la8garlic/Tahm/releases/tag/v0.9.0)

- A regular scan shows a cleanup suggestion and its reason above the treemap. Open the full list to filter by category or browse subsequent pages. Choose **Scan for suggestions** from Scan center to go directly to the list.
- Each suggestion explains why the item was flagged and what to check before removal. For example, verify a second complete copy of an archive or confirm that an installer is no longer needed for repair. Moving queued items to the Recycle Bin requires confirmation.
- The scanner reuses metadata from the open file handle. Disk usage and reclaim estimates continue to account for hard links.
- The title bar shows the running version. The application and Windows executable share the same disk analysis icon.

Download the ZIP and its SHA256SUMS.txt, then follow the [startup guide](../../README.en.md). WebView2 Runtime is required. The EXE ships without an Authenticode signature.
